1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal information you disclose to us

In Short: We collect personal information that you provide to us. We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and Services, when you participate in activities on the Services, or otherwise when you contact us. Personal Information Provided by You. The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the Services, the choices you make, and the products and features you use. The personal information we collect may include the following: • names • phone numbers • passwords and login PINs • business name and business type • business address, city, state and PIN code • email address and website (optional) • business tax and licence identifiers you choose to add to your invoices, such as GSTIN, PAN and FSSAI number (optional) • your UPI ID and UPI QR code image, if you choose to add one so your customers can pay you • your business logo and the item, menu and product photos you upload • the business records you enter, such as items, prices, stock, bills, expenses, vendor bills and credit (udhaar) entries All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes to such personal information.

Sensitive Information

We do not process sensitive information such as racial or ethnic origin, political or religious beliefs, sexual orientation, health data, biometric data, or genetic data. We do not collect or store card numbers, CVVs, net-banking credentials, UPI PINs, or bank passwords at any point. The business tax and licence identifiers you add to your invoices — GSTIN, PAN and FSSAI number — are stored because they are printed on GST-compliant bills. They are used for that purpose only.

Information you enter about other people (your customers and staff)

In Short: Vittam stores the customer and staff details you enter, on your behalf and on your instructions. Vittam is a tool you use to run your own business, so some of the information you enter is about other people: • Customer details on bills. When you record a bill you may add a customer name and phone number. These are saved with that bill so you can look up customer history, maintain a credit (udhaar) ledger, and send the bill to that customer over WhatsApp when you ask us to. • Online store and QR order details. If you switch on your online store or table QR ordering, your customers enter their own name, phone number (which they verify with a one-time password), a delivery address if they choose delivery, and any note they add to the order. If they pay you by UPI, the transaction reference they enter is stored against the order so you can match the payment. • Staff details. If you create logins for your staff, the name and login PIN you set are stored so those people can sign in, and significant actions taken in your account are recorded in an activity log. You decide what to enter, and you are responsible for having the right to share it with us and for telling your customers and staff how their information is used. For this information we act only as a processor on your instructions: we store and process it to provide Vittam to you, and we do not use it for our own purposes, do not sell it, and do not share it with anyone for marketing.

Information automatically collected

In Short: Some information — such as your IP address, device characteristics, and activity in the app — is collected automatically when you use our Services. We automatically collect certain information when you visit, use, or navigate the Services. This information does not reveal your specific identity on its own, but may include: • Device and connection information, such as your device model, operating system and version, app version, language, and whether you are online or offline. • Log and security data, such as your IP address and the browser or device identifier (user agent) recorded when you sign in. We use this to detect suspicious sign-ins, to apply rate limits against abuse, and to investigate problems. • Activity records. We keep an audit log of significant actions in your account — for example sign-ins, price changes, cancelled bills, staff changes and account deletion requests — so that you and we can see what happened and when. • A push notification token, issued by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for each device you sign in on, so that order and service notifications can be delivered to that device. We use this information to keep the Services secure and working, for troubleshooting, and for internal analysis in aggregate. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell it.

Photos you upload, and what your customers can see

In Short: If you switch on your online store, the photos and prices you upload are meant to be seen by your customers. The item, menu and product photos you upload, your business logo, and your UPI QR image are stored on our servers and shown inside the app, on your menu, and on your bills. If you enable your online store or table QR ordering, they are also shown on your public store page, which anyone with your store link or QR code can open — along with your business name, address, item names and prices. That is the purpose of the feature. Please do not upload images that contain personal or confidential information you would not want your customers to see. You can replace or delete any uploaded image, or switch the online store off, at any time from within the app. When you choose to send a bill to a customer over WhatsApp, that bill is published at a unique, randomly generated web link so the customer can open it without signing in. Anyone who has that exact link can view that one bill, which shows the bill contents and your business details. The link cannot be guessed and is not indexed by search engines, but please treat it like any other private link.

Payments

We do not process payments between you and your customers, and we never see or store their card or UPI credentials. Payments happen directly — in cash, or through your own UPI app using the UPI ID or QR code you configure in Vittam. For online orders we may store the UPI transaction reference your customer types in, and the payment status you or they mark, purely as part of the order record.

Information stored on your own device

Vittam is built to keep working when the internet does not. Your recent business data — items, prices, bills created while offline, and similar records — is stored in a database on your own device and synced to our servers once you are back online. Your login session and PIN-related data are held in your device's secure storage. This local copy stays on the device until it has synced and you sign out or uninstall the app. On the public online store page, your customers' own browsers save their name, phone number, delivery address and a short-lived order session locally, so they do not have to type them again on their next order. Clearing the browser data removes them.

Application Data

If you use our application(s), we also may collect the following information if you choose to provide us with access or permission: • Bluetooth, and the Location permission it requires. We request Bluetooth permissions so the app can find, pair with, and print to your thermal printer. On Android, the operating system also requires the location permission before any app is allowed to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices, so Vittam asks for it at the point where you set up a printer. We do not collect, store, or transmit your location, and Vittam has no location-based features. You can refuse or revoke this permission in your device's settings; the only thing that stops working is discovering Bluetooth printers. • Mobile Device Access. We may request access or permission to certain features from your mobile device, including your mobile device's bluetooth, camera, photos and media, and other features. If you wish to change our access or permissions, you may do so in your device's settings. • Camera and Photos. We request access to your camera and photo library only when you choose to add a picture — for example, a photo of an item or menu product, or your UPI QR code image. You pick the source (take a photo or choose an existing one) each time, and only the image you select is uploaded to our servers so it can be shown in your store, menu, and bills. We do not access your camera in the background and we do not scan or read other photos in your gallery. You can revoke camera or photo access at any time in your device's settings; the rest of the app continues to work without it. • Push Notifications. We may request to send you push notifications regarding your account or certain features of the application(s). If you wish to opt out from receiving these types of communications, you may turn them off in your device's settings. This information is primarily needed to maintain the security and operation of our application(s), for troubleshooting, and for our internal analytics and reporting purposes.

Google API

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. DO WE USE COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES?

In Short: We use a small amount of browser storage to keep a session working and to remember what was typed. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. Our website, our online store pages and our app do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, third-party tracking pixels, or session-recording tools. We use only what is needed to make the Services work: • On the online store and QR ordering pages, your customer's browser keeps a short-lived session token after their phone number is verified, together with the name, phone number and delivery address they entered, so they do not have to type them again for their next order. Clearing the browser data removes all of it. • In the mobile and desktop app, the equivalent session information is stored in your device's own secure storage rather than in cookies. We do not currently use cookies or similar technologies to profile you or to serve advertising. If that ever changes, we will update this notice and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

In Short: We keep your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Notice unless otherwise required by law. We will only keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (such as tax, accounting, or other legal requirements). No purpose in this notice will require us keeping your personal information for longer than the period of time in which users have an account with us. When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal information, we will either delete or anonymize such information, or, if this is not possible (for example, because your personal information has been stored in backup archives), then we will securely store your personal information and isolate it from any further processing until deletion is possible. In practice this means: • We keep your account, your business records, and the customer and staff details you entered for as long as your account is active. • If you ask us to delete your account, the deletion is scheduled 30 days ahead so that you have time to cancel it if you change your mind. After that window your account and its business data are erased from our active systems, and copies in routine backups age out on their normal rotation. • We may keep a limited record for longer where we have to: the fact that a deletion request was made and acted on, logs showing that a one-time password was sent, and any records that tax, GST, accounting or other laws require us to retain, or that we need to resolve a dispute, investigate fraud, or enforce our terms. • The copy of your data cached on your own device is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

6. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

In Short: We aim to protect your personal information through a system of organizational and technical security measures. We have implemented appropriate and reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, despite our safeguards and efforts to secure your information, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise or guarantee that hackers, cybercriminals, or other unauthorized third parties will not be able to defeat our security and improperly collect, access, steal, or modify your information. Although we will do our best to protect your personal information, transmission of personal information to and from our Services is at your own risk. You should only access the Services within a secure environment. The measures we use include encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all traffic between the app and our servers, storage of login PINs and passwords in hashed form rather than as plain text, signed session tokens with expiry, rate limiting on sensitive endpoints such as sign-in and one-time passwords, separation of each business's data so one account cannot read another's, restricted staff access on a need-to-know basis, and audit logging of significant account actions. You can help by choosing a PIN that is not easy to guess, not sharing it, giving staff their own logins instead of your own, and signing out on devices you no longer use.

10. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.
