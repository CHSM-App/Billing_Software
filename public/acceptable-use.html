This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") is part of the Vittam Terms of Service. It applies to every business using Vittam and to everyone who logs in under its Account, including Staff. Words with capitals have the meaning given in the Terms of Service.

Breaking this Policy is a breach of the Terms of Service.

1. What you must not do

You must not use Vittam, or let anyone use your Account, to do any of the following.

1.1 Illegal activity

  • Break any law that applies to you or your business.
  • Sell goods or services that are illegal, or regulated goods (for example liquor, tobacco, medicines or other licensed items) without the licence or permission the law requires.

1.2 Tax misuse

  • Create fake, inflated or backdated GST invoices, or invoices for supplies that did not happen.
  • Use the Service to evade tax, or to help someone else evade it.
  • Deliberately misuse the tax features to produce misleading invoices or returns.

1.3 Content you have no rights to

  • Upload logos, photos, menus, brand names or other content that you do not own and do not have permission to use.
  • Use another business's name or branding to pass yourself off as that business.

1.4 Unlawful content on public pages

  • Put content on your online store or QR menu pages that is unlawful, defamatory, obscene, hateful, or that deceives customers (for example false prices or descriptions).

1.5 Spam and messaging abuse

  • Send bills, messages or OTPs through the Service to people who have not dealt with your business or who have asked you to stop.
  • Use the Service's WhatsApp or other messaging features for bulk or unsolicited promotional messages, or in breach of the messaging provider's rules.

1.6 Misuse of personal data

  • Collect customer or staff personal data through the Service for a purpose other than running your business with them, or beyond what you have told them.
  • Sell, share or publish your Customers' personal data obtained through the Service.

1.7 Licence and security circumvention

To the extent the law allows us to prohibit it, you must not:

  • reverse engineer, decompile or modify the Application;
  • bypass or tamper with the licence check, Subscription status, offline limits or plan limits (such as Staff or device limits).

1.8 Attacks on the Service

  • Scrape, crawl or extract data from the Service or its public pages by automated means, except through features we provide for that.
  • Overload the Service, or send traffic designed to slow it down or make it unavailable.
  • Probe, scan or test the Service for vulnerabilities without our written permission. (If you find a security problem, please report it to support@vengurlatech.com. We welcome responsible reports.)
  • Introduce malware or harmful code.

1.9 Account misuse

  • Share one Account between separate, unrelated businesses where your plan does not allow it.
  • Resell, rent or sub-license access to the Service, or give access to anyone outside your business, without our written agreement.
  • Try to access another business's Account, devices or data, or another person's login.

2. How we enforce this Policy

2.1 If we reasonably believe this Policy has been broken, we may, depending on how serious the breach is:

  1. warn you and ask you to fix it;
  2. require corrective action, for example removing content from your public pages;
  3. suspend your Account or a feature (such as your online store) until it is fixed;
  4. terminate your Account for serious or repeated breaches.

2.2 Usually we will contact you first and give you a reasonable chance to fix the problem. We may suspend immediately, without advance notice, if there is a serious security incident, apparent illegal activity, a risk of harm to others, or a legal requirement. We will tell you the reason as soon as we can.

2.3 We may report illegal activity to the relevant authorities, and we will cooperate with lawful requests from them.

2.4 Enforcement is not automatic. We decide each case on its facts, as fairly as we can. [[PENDING: advocate to confirm the enforcement process and any notice requirements]]

3. Reporting a breach, and asking for a review

3.1 Report a breach. If you see Vittam being used in breach of this Policy (for example a store page using your brand without permission), email support@vengurlatech.com with the link or details and why you believe it breaks this Policy.

3.2 Ask for a review. If we have acted against your Account and you believe we got it wrong, email support@vengurlatech.com with your business name, registered phone number and an explanation. We will review it and reply within [[PENDING: timeline]].
