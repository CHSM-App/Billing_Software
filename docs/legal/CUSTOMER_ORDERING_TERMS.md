These terms apply when you order from a shop or restaurant through its **online store** or a **table QR menu** that runs on Vittam.

## 1. Who you are buying from

- **The shop is the seller.** Your order is with the shop or restaurant whose page you are on. The shop decides its menu, prices and availability. It is responsible for preparing, packing and delivering your order, and for any applicable taxes and food-safety requirements.
- **Vittam provides the software.** Vittam is ordering and billing software made by Vengurla Tech Private Limited [[PENDING: verify entity name]]. Vittam is not the seller of the shop's goods or services.

## 2. Your order

- The shop may accept or reject your order. An order is confirmed only when the shop accepts it.
- Prices, photos and descriptions on the page come from the shop. If you have a question about an item, ask the shop.

## 3. Payment

- If you pay by UPI, your payment goes **directly to the shop**, using the UPI details the shop provides. Vittam does not receive or hold your money.
- If you type in a UPI transaction reference, the shop checks it against its own payment records.

## 4. Cancellations, returns and refunds

- The shop's own cancellation, return and refund rules apply to your order. Ask the shop about them before you order.
- Any refund for an order is made by the shop.
- Nothing in these terms limits your rights under consumer protection law.

## 5. Your personal data

- To place an order you give your name and mobile number, verified by a one-time password (OTP). For delivery you also give an address. You may add a note.
- The shop uses these details to process and deliver your order and to contact you about it. Vittam stores them on the shop's behalf so the ordering system works. See the [Vittam Privacy Policy](/privacy) for how Vittam handles this data.

## 6. Questions and complaints

- **About your order** (items, delivery, payment or refund): contact the shop directly.
- **About the Vittam ordering pages themselves** (for example a page that does not work, or misuse of the platform): email **support@vengurlatech.com**. Grievance details are in section 21 of the [Vittam Terms of Service](/terms).
